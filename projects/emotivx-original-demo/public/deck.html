Stage 2: Physics Interpolation

Turning data clouds into data art

The Problem

Raw data is sparse and ugly:

Event 1: Pass A → B (2 points)
Event 2: Carry by B (2 points)
Event 3: Shot B → goal (2 points)
Total: 6 sparse points = 3 straight lines

Looks choppy. Not beautiful. Won't work on a hoodie.

The Solution

Physics-based interpolation

  1. Physics simulation (ball trajectory, player movement)
  2. Spline smoothing (cubic/Bezier curves)
  3. Aesthetic tuning (optimize for BEAUTY, not just accuracy)

Output: 100-300 smooth points → looks natural and beautiful

Key Innovation

We optimize for BEAUTY,
not just accuracy.

95% data fidelity
100% visual appeal
